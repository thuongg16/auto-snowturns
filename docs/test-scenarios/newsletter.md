# Newsletter Test Scenarios

These scenarios cover the newsletter email subscription feature on Automation Exercise, based on direct verification of the live application (subscription form present on the home page and the cart page). They define **what** should be tested; step-by-step actions and expected results belong to the Test Case phase.

## 1. Subscription

### NEWS-S01 — Subscribe to the newsletter with a valid email on the home page
Verifies that submitting a valid email address in the home page's "SUBSCRIPTION" section results in a success confirmation.

### NEWS-S02 — Subscribe to the newsletter with a valid email on the cart page
Verifies that the same subscription functionality is available and works correctly on the cart page.

### NEWS-S03 — Attempt to subscribe with an invalid email format
Verifies that the subscription form does not accept a malformed email address.
